Concept design

Money transfer, West Africa only

A clickable mobile prototype of the transfer flow. Sending is limited to the eight West African markets where Orange Money operates. Every other destination is out of scope by design.

  1. Home: balance and recent transfers
  2. Destination: the eight West African countries
  3. Recipient: local number format per country
  4. Amount: fee, rate and payout shown before you commit
  5. Review, then confirm with your PIN
  6. Receipt with a transfer reference

Rates, fees and limits are placeholder values for the prototype. Demo PIN: any 4 digits.
