Concept · Mobile money

A money transfer app for West Africa

An interactive prototype of an Orange Money–style wallet, limited to the West African markets where Orange runs mobile money. It covers sending within one country, sending across borders inside the UEMOA (CFA franc) zone, and sending to markets with their own currency, where the app shows the exchange rate.

Unofficial concept design for study only. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Orange. Fees, rates and limits are sample values, and no real transactions or PINs are handled.

Design principles

  • Country first. Picking the destination comes first, so the dial code, currency, language and number format are right from the start.
  • Show the full cost. The fee, exchange rate and amount received are all visible before the PIN step, with nothing added later.
  • Built for low-end phones. Large tap targets, high contrast, a short flow of 4 steps, and no heavy imagery.
  • Multilingual. French, English and Portuguese labels match each market's official language. Wolof, Bambara, Mooré and other local languages could be added later.
  • Familiar mental model. The flow follows the USSD menu (#144#), so people moving from feature phones recognise each step.
