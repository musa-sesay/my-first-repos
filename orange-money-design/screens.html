Rules

  • Tap targets are at least 44 px, and main buttons sit within thumb reach.
  • Every amount shows its currency code. XOF and GNF have no decimals; SLE and LRD use 2.
  • A country flag always appears next to a cross-border contact.
  • Number grouping follows the locale: 25 000 in French, 25,000 in English.
  • Screens are light enough for low-end Android phones, with no photos in the core flows.
